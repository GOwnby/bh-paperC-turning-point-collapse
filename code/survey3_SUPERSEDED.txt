survey3.json and survey3.log are SUPERSEDED. They are the output of survey3.py as shipped in the original archive (Zenodo v1.0.1), unchanged, and they are NOT the data of the paper.
The paper's survey table (Table 2 and the turning-point table of Sec. 3.7) is survey4.json / survey4.log (survey4.py), regenerated by verify.sh.
The two disagree: for the ideal electron gas survey3 gives M_max = 1.41625 Msun and sigma^2 = 0 at rho_c = 2.3132e13 kg/m^3, survey4 gives M_max = 1.41661 Msun (the value of the paper) and sigma^2 = 0 at 2.3107e13 kg/m^3.
survey3.* is not read by any script or check of this repository. The reason for the difference has not been established here and survey3.log is not claimed to be reproducible with the present code.
